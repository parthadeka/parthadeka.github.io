Partha Pratim Deka

Joint Postdoctoral Researcher • UCT & SARAO
Astronomer on Duty

I am a radio astronomer specializing in data-intensive astronomy and the multi-wavelength characterization of radio sources. I recently completed my Ph.D. at IUCAA, Pune, where I studied cold atomic gas associated with radio-loud AGNs using the MeerKAT Absorption Line Survey (MALS).

Research Interests

  • Data intensive astronomy
    • Radio survey planning, catalog generation and characterization
    • Multi-wavelength characterization of radio sources
    • Applications of machine learning in astronomy
  • Galaxy evolution
    • Kinematics and distribution of cold gas in galaxies and its redshift evolution
    • Active Galactic Nuclei (AGN) feeding and feedback
    • High redshift radio galaxies
